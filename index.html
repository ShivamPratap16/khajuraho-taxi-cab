FAQ

Before you book

How far is Khajuraho airport and railway station?

The airport is about 6 km and the railway station about 8 km from the temples. Pickup takes 15–20 minutes.

Can you take us to Panna for the jungle safari?

Yes. We drop you at Madla gate in time for your safari slot, wait, and bring you back. Safari jeep tickets are booked separately on the MP Forest website.

Is the fare per person?

No, the fare is for the whole car, including fuel and driver.

Do you drive to Orchha, Jhansi, Varanasi or Agra?

Yes, one way or return. Ask on WhatsApp for the fare from your hotel.

How do I pay?

Pay the driver in cash or UPI at the end of the trip. For long tours a small advance confirms your booking.
